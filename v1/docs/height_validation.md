## Summary

- **Independent references:** 10 published heights (`docs/height_reference.csv`), of which 10 match a footprint. The acceptance target is at least 30, so **the count target is not met**: open web sources give heights only for tall landmarks (towers, spires, monuments) and none for ordinary buildings. More references have to come from the project owner (PLAN.md C16).
- **Tolerance:** within 3 m or 15%, whichever is larger. Required: at least 80% of references.
- **Automated pipeline height vs references:** 5/10 within tolerance (50%), mean absolute error 6.4 m, bias -2.4 m. This is the figure that measures the pipeline: it is the height before the curated landmark override below, and no reference is used to produce it.
- **Landmark override:** 12 landmarks are hand-listed in `config/landmarks.csv` and drawn as a body plus a stepped tower. Ten of them are the same buildings as the references, so their displayed heights match the references by construction. That makes them look right on the map but is **not** validation.
- **LiDAR-only height vs references:** 5/10 within tolerance, mean absolute error 7.4 m, bias -2.4 m.
- **Every building has a positive height:** 6207 buildings, 0 missing, 0 non-positive, minimum 1.0 m.

## Method

Roof level is the 99th percentile of the 0.5 m DSM inside the footprint. Ground level is the 10th percentile of the DTM in a 3 m ring around the footprint. Height is roof minus ground. A reference point is matched to the largest footprint within 5 m of its coordinates; this rule was fixed before looking at results.

Reference heights are mostly to the **tip of a spire or tower**, whereas the pipeline measures a roof level that suits floor-area allocation. That difference is deliberate and is the main reason slender landmarks are under-measured (see the sweep).

## Known limitations

- **Slender towers and spires** on wide footprints are under-measured, by design (roof level, not tip).
- **Valley edges:** a building above a cutting (Balmoral Hotel above Waverley station) gets a ground level from the low side, so its height is over-stated. Such sites are flagged `steep_terrain` and given medium confidence.
- **Trees** overhanging roofs raise the DSM, and demolished or new buildings since capture are not reflected.
- **Capture date:** Phase 5 was flown in 2020 to 2022 at programme level; the date over Edinburgh is not confirmed (spike S2).
- **Reference set** is small and biased toward tall landmarks. Statements about ordinary buildings rest on the OSM cross-check only.
