# Inferred buildings

Generated by `pipeline/stage02b_infill.py`. Do not edit by hand.

## What this is

Some ground in the full area has no building, street, railway, water, park or car park on it according to OpenStreetMap and the footprint data. This stage looks at what the LiDAR sees there and adds buildings of two kinds. **They are inferred, not mapped**: the map draws them in a lighter tone, they are kept out of the `buildings` table, and no census population is allocated to them until that is decided (PLAN.md A11).

| other ground left empty (courtyards, lone patches, small remnants) | 21625.21 |

Parks, gardens, water, railways, car parks, plazas and streets are never filled: OpenStreetMap says what they are. Trees and steep ground are recognised from the LiDAR and left alone, so the slopes of the Castle and Calton Hill stay open.

## How the standard buildings are made

- Ground within 12 m of a street, within 30 m of a mapped building, flat (under 1.5 m above the ground model) and not steeper than 25% is split into plots about 12 m wide with 0.4 m between them.
- Height is the median of mapped buildings within 60 m (those at least 4 m tall, landmarks excluded), kept between 3 and 7 storeys of 3.2 m. Every tunable is in `config/model.yaml` under `infill`.
- Interior courtyards and lone patches are left empty. Output is deterministic: no randomness is used.

## Limitations

- The LiDAR was flown in 2020 to 2022. Ground that was bare then may have been built on since, and structures seen then may be gone.
- Bare flat ground that OSM does not label may really be open (a yard, a lot, a lawn). The standard buildings there are a visual assumption, and `infill.fill_open_ground: false` removes them while keeping the LiDAR-detected structures.
- Street widths come from OSM centre lines and per-class half-widths, so narrow lanes and wide boulevards are approximate.
