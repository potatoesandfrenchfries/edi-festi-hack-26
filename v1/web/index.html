About these numbers

Every number is a modelled estimate. Nothing here is live or counted.

What the numbers are
  • Template values. People per building are placeholders proportional to floor area, with a use class read from map tags. They are not census figures. Only the city totals are scaled to the 2022 census.
  • Placeholder curves. The hourly shapes are drafted, not fitted to data, and are uncalibrated.
What is not modelled
  • Visitors, tourists, pupils and students.
  • Public holidays and festival days, other than the manual festival multiplier.
  • Inferred buildings have no people estimate at all.
Simulated buses
  • Not live. Each Lothian route has a bus every few minutes, looping end to end at the running times in the published timetable. Positions are computed, not received from buses.
  • Frequency comes from one typical weekday, so it ignores peaks, evenings and weekends. Buses do not react to traffic.
  • Credits. Timetables and route shapes: DfT Bus Open Data Service (OGL v3.0).
Cost of traffic
  • Time lost is the time cars and buses spend below the speed they would choose on a clear road, times the people on board and a value of time per hour. It is an opportunity cost, not takings.
  • Fuel and carbon come from litres burnt standing still and driving, a carbon factor per litre and a value per tonne. Bus fuel type is not in the timetable data, so diesel is assumed.
  • Scale. The simulated cars are a sample of the traffic. Every constant is a placeholder in config/economics.yaml until checked against published values.
Heights and data
  • Heights. From LiDAR, with curated towers for a dozen landmarks. Accuracy is still being checked.
  • Credits. Buildings: © OpenStreetMap contributors and Overture Maps. Heights: LiDAR for Scotland (OGL v3.0). Basemap: OpenFreeMap, © OpenMapTiles, data from OpenStreetMap.
