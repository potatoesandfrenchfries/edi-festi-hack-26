    About these numbers

    Every number is a modelled estimate. Nothing here is live or counted.

    What the numbers are
    • Template values. People per building are placeholders proportional to floor area, with a use class read from map tags. They are not census figures. Only the city totals are scaled to the 2022 census.
    • Placeholder curves. The hourly shapes are drafted, not fitted to data, and are uncalibrated.
    What is not modelled
    • Visitors, tourists, pupils and students.
    • Public holidays and festival days, other than the manual festival multiplier.
    • Inferred buildings have no people estimate at all.
    Heights and data
    • Heights. From LiDAR, with curated towers for a dozen landmarks. Accuracy is still being checked.
